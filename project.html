About Us

God's Care Agricultral Farm is Farming project ran by NABIRYE MARY committed to producing healthy poultry products and animal products through modern farming techniques and proper animal care.

We supply different breeds of animals, quality chicken, and Agricultral farming support to homes, businesses, and local communities.
